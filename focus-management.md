# Learning Process - Assignment

## Question 1

**What is Deep Work?**
Deep Work is the ability to focus deeply on a task without distractions. It produces high-quality results in less time and improves learning, creativity, and productivity.

---

## Question 2

**According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?**

1. Work in distraction-free environments.
2. Schedule fixed time blocks for deep work.
3. Prioritize most important tasks first.
4. Take breaks to recharge the brain.
5. Reduce shallow work (emails, social media, unimportant meetings).

---

## Question 3

**How can you implement the principles in your day to day life?**

1. Plan 2–3 deep work sessions daily.
2. Turn off phone/notifications while working.
3. Use a to-do list to separate deep work from shallow tasks.
4. Set clear start and end times.
5. Review progress weekly.

---

## Question 4

**What are the dangers of social media, in brief?**

1. Reduces focus and attention span.
2. Creates addiction and wastes time.
3. Increases stress, anxiety, and comparison with others.
4. Weakens ability to do deep, meaningful work.
5. Makes the brain crave constant stimulation.
