B = Behaviour
M = Motivation
A = Ablity
P = Prompt.

1. Shrink the habit into its tiniest form.

The Story of the photography class (Quantity vs. Quality). The professor split his class into two groups. The Quantity group was evaluated based on the number of photos they took (100 photos = A, 90 photos = B, etc.). The Quality group was evealuated based on producing just one perfect photo. ## Question 6

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?**

According to the book, making a habit easier is the third law of behavior change. Reducing the friction makes it far more likely to do the thing or adapt the habit.

---

## Question 7

**Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?**

In order to break the bad habits, according to the book, we need to make our bad habits more difficult by making the cues invisible, the action unattractive and hard and the reward unsatisfying.

I would like to be fit physically. ## Question 9

**Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of? What are the steps that you can take to make it make the cue invisible or the process unattractive or hard or the response unsatisfying?**

One habit I would like to eliminate is mindlessly scrolling through social media before falling asleep. To make the cue invisible, I will charge my phone in a different room or across the room, removing the visual trigger from my bedside. I will make the action hard by deleting the apps from my home screen or using a website blocker that activates after 10 PM, adding significant friction to the process. Finally, to make the response unsatisfying, I will keep a sleep log; seeing the clear link between late-night scrolling and poor energy the next day will highlight the negative consequence.
